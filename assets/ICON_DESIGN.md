Subject: a bold geometric folder silhouette merged with a compact server shape and two opposing transfer arrows, forming a distinctive single coherent emblem. Style: clean high-tech flat icon with restrained dimensional shading, crisp broad shapes, strong silhouette readable at 32 pixels. No text, letters, wordmarks, tiny detail, badges, scenery, logos of other programs, or multiple icon variants. Produce the final icon artwork suitable for conversion to a Windows ICO.
